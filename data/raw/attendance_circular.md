**Notice ID:** ATT-2026-01  
**Status:** Fictional RuleLens test source

## 1. Regular Examination Eligibility

For the 2026 academic year, a student must maintain at least **80% attendance** in a course to be eligible for the regular end-semester examination.

Documented medical absence may be considered according to departmental procedure.

This circular intentionally differs from Section 5.1 of the main regulations, which states a 75% threshold.
