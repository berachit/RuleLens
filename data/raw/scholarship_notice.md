# Demo University — Merit Scholarship Notice 2026

**Notice ID:** SCH-2026-01  
**Award cycle:** 2026  
**Status:** Fictional RuleLens test source

## 1. Scope

This notice concerns the Demo University Merit Scholarship for undergraduate students.

## 2. Eligibility

An undergraduate student must have a cumulative GPA of **at least 8.50/10.00** and must have no active disciplinary penalty at the time of award.

## 3. Application

Applications must be submitted through the designated scholarship portal by the deadline stated in the scholarship notice. Applications submitted after the deadline are ordinarily not considered.

This notice intentionally differs from Section 9.2 of the main regulations, which states an 8.00/10.00 threshold.
